COMPANY PROFILE

OM TRADING, technical solutions for the building industry.

OM TRADING is an Algerian company specializing in two complementary activities: technical and special door solutions, representing ALPHA STONE in Algeria, and the supply, processing and installation of marble, granite and quartz.

We support project owners, contractors, engineering firms, architects and professionals from needs assessment through supply and installation, with particular attention to quality, technical compliance and project follow-up.
